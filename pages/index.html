A lost duck is trying to get home, and a ferret in a stovepipe hat schemes against her.

A pixel-art platformer. Zone 1 is a New York street with red contact edges.

Play
  • Touch: drag left or right to walk, and press Jump or Use.
  • Keyboard: arrow keys to walk and jump, E to use, P to pause.

Add app to Home screen

Tap the menu, then More, then Add app to Home screen.

Add to Home Screen

Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
